Dress Code

We invite you to celebrate with us in either traditional or formal attire. Colorful clothing is encouraged!

Traditional Áo Dài

Wear the elegant traditional Vietnamese áo dài. This beautiful long tunic dress is perfect for honoring Vietnamese culture and adds a special touch to our celebration. Áo dài can be tailored or borrowed for this occasion.

Formal Attire

Elegant evening wear such as formal dresses, suits, and cocktail attire are all appropriate choices. We suggest choosing colors that make you feel confident and beautiful.

✨ A Colorful Celebration

We encourage our guests to wear vibrant colors and express their personal style! Whether traditional or formal, we'd love to see the colors of celebration. However, colorful attire is not required—wear what makes you feel elegant and comfortable.
